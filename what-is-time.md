# What Is Time?

_Notes from a conversation, October 2026. From Aristotle's number of motion to the railway telegraph to the Wheeler–DeWitt equation to the entropy cost of reading a clock._

***

## 1. A working definition

**Time is a correlation between a clock subsystem and the rest of the world. It is a parameter only in regimes where some subsystem behaves as a good clock or the state is locally thermal. The time that more than one system can share (a definite tick, a definite duration, a definite order) is a set of records, and records cost entropy.**

The first clause is Page–Wootters (1983) and Rovelli's relational observables. The second is the semiclassical approach (Banks; Halliwell–Hawking) and Connes–Rovelli's thermal time (1994). The third is the 2025 thermodynamics of timekeeping (Meier et al.; Ares's group at Oxford), with _Measurement_'s record and Augustine's present of past things behind it. The intuition had two pictures: time as **flow**, something passing whose present alone exists, and time as **number**, something counted against a regular motion.

Working claim broken by the genealogy: **time is the parameter of change: one external order, the same for everything, in which the world evolves.** It fails three times: time stops being one, stops being external, and the clock stops being free (§6).

The question held in view was the one _Space_ handed on: **can the observer–world cut be derived, or only assumed?** In stage 5a it sharpened to: **does physics pick out where records form?**

Distinctions worth keeping:

* **Flow vs. number.** Heraclitus, Plotinus, and Augustine against Aristotle and the escapement. Physics took the number; the flow returned as the arrow.
* **Temporal vs. equal hours.** A fraction of daylight against a fixed quantity of a uniform mechanism. The unit changed when the mechanism was allowed to define it.
* **Relative vs. conventional.** Simultaneity is relative to a reference system and determined by causal structure once the system is fixed (Malament). It is not a free convention (Reichenbach).
* **Proper time vs. being a clock.** Proper time is the length of a worldline: geometry. Whether a physical system reads it is a fact about its dynamics: the clock hypothesis.
* **Law vs. state.** No global time by law; cosmic time is selected by the state. Thermal time puts the flow itself in the state.
* **Ticking vs. recording.** The clockwork can be nearly reversible (ring clock); turning a tick into a shared classical record dominates the cost (double quantum dot, 2025).
* **Shared vs. unshared time.** Unshared time can be coherent, superposed, causally indefinite. Shared time is definite, irreversible, and costly.

Pattern: space began as a metaphysical posit (the void, so that things can be many). Time began as a practice of division (the hour) and was promoted to a metaphysical posit by Newton, who named whatever every physical clock was irregular relative to. (Position.)

## 2. The intuition — flow, number, measure

**Temporal hours.** Egyptian and Roman practice split daylight into twelve hours whatever its length. Worked: with 16 hours of daylight, a daytime hour is 80 minutes; with 8, it is 40. A clepsydra dripping at a constant rate needed a different scale for each month. The uniform process was bent to fit the convention.

**Parmenides, Heraclitus, Zeno.** Being _is_, all together, now; Heraclitus's authentic fragment is about what persists through flux, not the river slogan. Zeno's arrow: at each instant the arrow occupies a space equal to itself, so it is at rest; time is made of instants; so it never moves. Three answers: Aristotle (time is not composed of nows), Russell's at-at theory (motion is being at different places at different times), and phase space (the instant enriched with momentum). _Position:_ Russell answers the arrow for kinematics, phase space for dynamics. The arrow returns in its strongest form as the frozen formalism, and Page–Wootters answers it Russell's way.

**Plato** (_Timaeus_): time as the moving image of eternity, made _with_ the heavens; the planets mark its numbers. No time before the cosmos.

**Aristotle** (_Physics_ IV.10–14): time is the number of motion with respect to before and after. One time for everything, because a hundred horses and a hundred men have the same number: simultaneous motions are counted by the same time. Would there be time without a soul to count? _Position:_ the argument for a single time is valid and rests on a hidden premise, that we can tell which distant motions are simultaneous. Stage 2 removes it. The soul question is the earliest form of the cut between the counter and what it counts. Plotinus objected that Aristotle defined how time is measured, not what it is.

**Augustine** (_Confessions_ XI). "If no one asks me, I know." No time before creation. Three presents: memory, attention, expectation. We measure the impression the passing leaves: _distentio animi_. _Position:_ the right structure in the wrong place. The past exists only as present records. Physics moves the records out of the mind, copies them, and makes them asymmetric. That is the opening bet.

**Eternity and the beginning.** Philoponus against an actual infinity traversed; al-Ghazali and Averroes; Aquinas (neither side demonstrable). Boethius separates sempiternity (endless duration) from eternity (all at once), a precursor of the block view. Kant's first antinomy closes the dispute and hands the infinity question to _The continuum_.

**The escapement.** Su Song's tower (1088–92); the verge-and-foliot in Europe in the late 13th century; Salisbury (c. 1386); Dondi's astrarium (1364). The temporal hour gives way to the equal hour. Oresme (1377): the heavens as a wound clock. _Position:_ a more decisive step than Newton. The machine had made time uniform and divisible before anyone theorized it.

**Time as an axis.** The Merton mean speed theorem; Oresme's graphs; Galileo (1638). Worked: distances 1, 4, 9, 16 after 1, 2, 3, 4 units, so 1, 3, 5, 7 in each successive unit, the odd-number rule. Barrow, then Newton's fluents and fluxions: the calculus that answers Zeno is written in the flowing time Zeno doubted.

**The pendulum.** Huygens (1656; the cycloid, 1673): error from a quarter-hour a day to under a minute. Then the sun was shown irregular: the equation of time, sundial noon up to about 16 minutes fast (November) and 14 slow (February). Rømer (1676): Io's eclipses and the finite speed of light, the first timing of light between distant places.

**Harrison** (H4, 1761). Worked: local noon at 3 p.m. Greenwich time puts the ship 45° west. Time as a transported convention (_Measurement_). Transport looked flawless because no ship moved fast enough to show otherwise.

**Newton.** "Absolute, true, and mathematical time, of itself, and from its own nature, flows equably without relation to anything external." Justified in the same passage by the equation of time: perhaps no motion is equable. _Position:_ absolute time is the equation of time made a principle, and operationally it is **the parameter in which the laws hold** (Neumann 1870, Lange 1885, ephemeris time 1952). The law defines the time. Leibniz: time as the order of succession, the temporal shift argument; Leibniz right that instants have no identity, Newton right that the laws pick out a uniform time. Kant: the form of inner sense.

**What the intuition contains.** (1) One time for all, depending silently on simultaneity. (2) Time independent of every clock, defined in practice by the laws. (3) The instant as a slice. (4) The past as present traces. The first two are the working claim; the third returns as the frozen formalism; the fourth as the bet.

## 3. The dispute — what does it mean for distant clocks to agree?

**The network.** Bristol about ten minutes behind London. Great Western on London time (1840); the Railway Clearing House (1847); GMT legal in Britain (1880); North American standard time zones, the "day of two noons" (18 November 1883); the Meridian Conference (1884). Telegraphic longitude from the 1840s: signals both ways, transmission delay taken as half the round trip. **That averaging is the whole of the 1905 definition, practised for half a century before anyone asked whether it was a measurement or a choice.**

**Mach** (1883): time is an abstraction from the dependence of changes on one another. The method, not the replacement.

**Lorentz's local time** (1895), t′ = t − vx/c², a device to make Maxwell's equations take their rest form. Kept minimal; _Waves_ covered the electrodynamics.

**Poincaré**, "La mesure du temps" (1898). Two problems. Equality of durations: no intuition; choose the definition that makes the laws simplest. Distant simultaneity: no intuition; astronomers assume constant signal speed in all directions, a postulate untestable without already-synchronized clocks. In 1900 he showed that light-signal synchronization by co-moving observers yields Lorentz's local time. By 1905–06: the relativity principle, the Lorentz group, the invariant quadratic form. But he kept the aether, and with it a true time behind the apparent one.

**Einstein** (1905). A time statement is a statement of simultaneity with a clock reading. Clocks at A and B are synchronous **by definition** if t\_B − t\_A = t′\_A − t\_B. Worked (c = 1): a rod of length 1 at v = 0.6; out-trip 2.5, return 0.625, so the platform puts reflection at 80% of the round trip, not 50%. Flashes at x = ±3, t = 0: γ = 1.25 gives t′ = ∓2.25, simultaneous for one observer and 4.5 apart for the other. The interval survives: t² − x² = −9 in both frames. Coincidence remains absolute; uniform ticking is assumed.

**Priority.** _Position:_ Poincaré had every component. Einstein's contribution was subtraction: no true time behind the local one. The same move as in _Computation_, a definition replacing what it was introduced to approximate. The counterposition (Lorentz; Bell 1976; Brown 2005) is that contraction and dilation are dynamical; carried to stage 4 of this summary.

**Galison** (2003): patents and the Bureau des Longitudes. _Position:_ the context explains why the procedure looked natural; the causal claim about Einstein is unproven (Martínez 2004), and the argument does not need it.

**Conventionality.** Reichenbach (1928): t\_B = t\_A + ε(t′\_A − t\_A), any 0 < ε < 1; the one-way speed of light is not measurable. Slow clock transport (Ellis–Bowman 1967) agrees with ε = ½ but presupposes one-way speeds. Malament (1977): from causal structure plus an inertial worldline, the only nontrivial definable simultaneity is ε = ½ (with Sarkar–Stachel 1999 on time reflection). _Position:_ **Malament wins the question that matters. Simultaneity is relative, not conventional:** relative to a reference system, then fixed by physics. This makes the later claim that time is a relation to a clock something that has to be earned.

**Metaphysics in one paragraph.** McTaggart (1908); Rietdijk (1966) and Putnam (1967) for the block; Stein (1968): the only invariant present is the event itself. _Position, tentative:_ Rietdijk–Putnam smuggles a global present back in through transitivity. What survives is an indexical now, the status Everett gives outcomes.

**Settled:** time stops being one, in kinematic form. Absolute: coincidence, light-cone order, the interval. **Cost:** no global now; equality of durations postponed.

## 4. The formalization — proper time as length

**Minkowski** (1908): ds² = dt² − dx² − dy² − dz²; proper time τ = ∫√(1 − v²) dt, the Lorentzian length of a worldline, invariant. Equality of durations answered: equal lengths. The straight worldline is the _longest_. Worked: out and back at 0.8 over 4 light-years; each leg Δt = 5, Δx = 4, τ = √(25 − 16) = 3; traveller 6 years, Earth 10. _Position:_ the twin paradox is about path length, not acceleration. The turnaround is where the path bends, not the cause.

**The clock hypothesis.** Bell and Brown: clocks slow for dynamical reasons. The CERN muon ring (Bailey et al. 1977): γ ≈ 29.3, accelerations near 10¹⁸ g, lifetimes dilated by the speed factor alone to about 0.1%. _Position:_ **proper time is geometry; being a clock is dynamics.** The muon result confirms the muon, not the geometry. A clock is a physical subsystem that must meet conditions, the third move in classical form.

**General relativity.** dτ/dt ≈ 1 + Φ/c². Pound–Rebka (1959, 22.5 m, 2.5 × 10⁻¹⁵); Hafele–Keating (1971: −59 ns east, +273 ns west); optical clocks over 33 cm (2010) and about a millimetre within one sample (JILA 2022). Worked, GPS: speed −7.2 μs/day (v²/2c² ≈ 8.3 × 10⁻¹¹); altitude +45.6 μs/day (GM(1/R\_E − 1/r)/c² ≈ 5.3 × 10⁻¹⁰); net +38 μs/day, roughly 11 km of ranging error a day uncorrected; satellite clocks pre-set slow by about 4.5 parts in 10¹⁰.

**Cosmic time.** Static spacetimes (a timelike Killing field) and FRW cosmology give a preferred time; the CMB picks a rest frame; the universe is 13.8 billion years old in it. _Position:_ **Newton's absolute time returns as a property of the state, not the law.** Right about our universe, wrong about physics.

**Gödel** (1949): a rotating dust universe with closed timelike curves through every point; no global time; time's lapse ideal, as Kant held. Chronology protection (Hawking 1992) unsettled. _Position:_ the modal step fails: "the laws permit worlds without global time" does not show global time is not objective here, any more than rotational symmetry shows a crystal's orientation isn't objective. The real point: no global time is fixed by law. Irony for _mathematical-platonism.md_: Gödel, realist about sets, idealist about time.

**The vanishing Hamiltonian.** Dirac (1958), ADM (1959–62): spatial slices, lapse N and shift as Lagrange multipliers; H = ∫(N𝓗 + Nⁱ𝓗ᵢ) d³x with 𝓗 = 𝓗ᵢ = 0. Moving to the next slice is a change of slicing: **time evolution is gauge.** Worked: the parametrized particle, p\_t + H(q, p) = 0; quantized, (−iħ∂\_t + H)Ψ = 0, the Schrödinger equation as a constraint on a timeless state. Harmless there, because t is one variable appearing linearly; in gravity, 𝓗 is quadratic and no variable is singled out. _Position:_ **the vanishing Hamiltonian, the hole argument, and the absence of local gravitational energy are one fact** (Noether's second theorem). Exception at a boundary: ADM energy, a true Hamiltonian, read by a clock outside the dynamical region, the boundary Hamiltonian of _Space_.

**Settled:** durations are worldline lengths; time is one proper time per worldline. **Cost:** no global time by law, no local energy, and no time evolution except at a boundary. Time stops being external begins here.

## 5. The abstraction — time as a relation

### Canonical gravity

**Wheeler–DeWitt** (1967): ĤΨ = 0, Ψ a function on superspace. No t. **The frozen formalism** is Zeno's arrow for the universe.

**Kuchař (1992) and Isham (1993).** Three problems: global time (no clock variable monotonic everywhere), multiple choice (different clocks give inequivalent quantum theories), Hilbert space (which inner product). Three families: time before quantization (York time, dust clocks), time after quantization (semiclassical: Ψ ≈ e^{iS/ħ}χ, with S defining a time in which χ obeys Schrödinger's equation; Page–Wootters), no fundamental time (Rovelli, Barbour). _Position:_ the first family fails; the other two are compatible. **Fundamentally time is a relation; approximately a parameter, where some subsystem is a good clock**, as classical outcomes are a regime defined by decoherence.

**Relational observables** (Rovelli 1991, 2002; Dittrich 2006). Worked: for the parametrized free particle, Q(τ) = q + (τ − t)p is invariant under the constraint flow (q → q + εp, t → t + ε). With q = 2, p = 3, t = 1: Q(5) = 14. The trajectory recovered from a timeless gauge-invariant object. Periodic clocks break the recipe: the global time problem.

### Quantum clocks

**Pauli** (1933): a self-adjoint T conjugate to H would shift energies by any amount, below any ground state. No time operator for any Hamiltonian bounded below. What survives: Mandelstam–Tamm (1945) uncertainty as a system–clock relation; covariant time POVMs whose readings overlap; Salecker–Wigner (1958) mass limits. _Position:_ Pauli's theorem is about clocks: **no physical clock is ideal, and that is the price of the stability of matter.**

**Page–Wootters** (1983): (H\_C + H\_S)|Ψ⟩⟩ = 0; |ψ(t)⟩ = ⟨t|\_C|Ψ⟩⟩ obeys Schrödinger's equation. Worked: the history state (1/√2)(|0⟩\_C|ψ₀⟩ + |1⟩\_C U|ψ₀⟩), Feynman–Kitaev's clock register. With U = NOT and |ψ₀⟩ = |0⟩ it is a Bell state: relative to the clock the system flips; globally nothing changes; without the clock the system is maximally mixed. With U = identity, a product: the clock ticks and nothing happens. **Entanglement between clock and system measures change.**

**The factorization returns.** The same Bell vector is a product under another split of ℂ⁴ (_Space_ §5). _Position:_ **the problem of time is the factorization problem with one factor named "clock"**; the multiple choice problem is a choice of factorization.

**Kuchař's objections** (two-time questions; interaction; which clock). Giovannetti–Lloyd–Maccone (2015): put the apparatus and its memory in the timeless state; two-time questions become one-time questions about records, and the right propagators come back. Smith–Ahmadi (2019): interacting clocks give time-nonlocal conditional dynamics. _Position, tentative:_ the two-time objection is answered _only_ by records in the state. Augustine's present of past things, required for talking about more than one moment.

**The trinity** (Höhn–Smith–Lock 2021): Dirac relational observables, Page–Wootters conditional states, and the relational Schrödinger picture are unitarily equivalent, for a class of models including non-ideal clocks; not established for full gravity. _Position:_ canonical gravity's and quantum mechanics' answers are one answer in two notations. Different clocks are different quantum reference frames: **relative, not conventional**, as with simultaneity.

### Thermal time (construction in _modular-theory.md_)

**Modular flow.** For a faithful ρ, K = −log ρ and σ\_s(X) = ρ^{is} X ρ^{−is}; ρ is stationary. Worked: ρ = diag(0.8, 0.2), gap log 4, |0⟩⟨1| → 4^{is}|0⟩⟨1|; for a thermal state of H = diag(0, ε), βε = log 4, and modular flow is time evolution rescaled by β. Pure states have no flow; the maximally mixed state a trivial one. Without density matrices: Tomita's S = JΔ^{1/2} for a cyclic separating vector; Reeh–Schlieder gives every region its own flow. Takesaki: every faithful state is KMS for its own flow. Connes: the algebra carries a canonical flow up to inner transformations.

**Bisognano–Wichmann** (1975–76): the wedge's modular flow is a boost; T = a/2π, Unruh with no detector; about 4 × 10⁻²¹ K per m/s².

**Connes–Rovelli** (1994): physical time is the modular flow of the physical state. Passes: Gibbs states, Rindler, FRW (Rovelli 1993). Fails: two qubits at β = 1 and β = 2, K = H\_A + 2H\_B, so B's observables rotate twice as fast while the Hamiltonian runs both equally. _Position:_ **thermal time is correct where the state is locally thermal**, a regime. It also needs a division: a global pure state with the full algebra has no flow.

**Entropy as generator.** ⟨K⟩ = S. Positive-temperature KMS states are completely passive (Pusz–Woronowicz 1978). _Position, tentative:_ thermal time carries the second law's orientation, statically. It does not supply the bet's arrow: equilibrium makes no records.

### One answer with three components

In CLPW's de Sitter, H is the vacuum's modular Hamiltonian over the de Sitter temperature, and the closed-universe constraint H + H\_obs = 0 has the Page–Wootters form, with the observer's clock bounded below as Pauli requires. _Position:_ **canonical gravity says time must be relational; quantum clocks say relational to what; thermal time says along which flow. Thermal time is the time a clock would read if one were there, and the crossed product is what happens when one is.** Established for the static patch and black-hole exteriors, not as a general theorem.

## 6. The payoff — the clock as physical system, and as observer

### What a tick costs (2017–2025)

Precision N = ticks before the clock slips by one; cost Σ = entropy per tick in units of k\_B (Landauer's bit: 0.69 k\_B).

* **Linear bound.** Erker et al. (2017); Pearson et al. (2021, vibrating membrane, within a factor of ten of the bound); for fully dissipative clocks N ≤ Σ/2. Worked: N = 1,000 needs Σ ≥ 2,000 k\_B per tick, about 2,900 Landauer erasures.
* **Ring clock** (Meier et al., _Nature Physics_, June 2025): coherent transport round a spin ring, dissipation confined to one link, precision **exponential** in Σ. At Σ = 20, linear allows N ≤ 10; e^{cΣ} with c of order one allows hundreds of millions. The cost is not zero: clocks still need a flux toward equilibrium.
* **Reading the clock** (Ares group, _PRL_ 135, 200407, November 2025): in a double-quantum-dot clock, amplifying and recording ticks produced about a billion times the entropy of the clockwork. The factor is device-specific; the minimum cost of a quantum-to-classical tick is open.

_Position:_ **ticking need not be irreversible; a tick that can be shared must be.** A Page–Wootters clock costs nothing and is a clock only for what is entangled with it. Making its reading available to anything else is amplification into a record, and that is where the entropy goes. _Measurement_'s record, _Entropy_'s Landauer, and redundancy as objectivity converge on one price. The 4a records-in-the-state position now has a measured cost.

### Clocks in superposition

Zych–Costa–Pikovski–Brukner (2011). Worked: visibility V = |cos(ωΔτ/2)|; ωΔτ = 0 gives full interference, π gives none; the clock's proper time is a which-path record. Proposals: Paczos–Foo–Zych (_Quantum_, 2025), a trapped atom held at two heights reading a superposition of proper times; "Quantum signatures of proper time in optical ion clocks" (_PRL_, April 2026), with squeezed motional states. Quantum time dilation for velocity superpositions (Smith–Ahmadi), about 10⁻¹⁵ at 10 m/s. **Not yet observed as of October 2026 searches.** _Position:_ geometry assigns each path a definite proper time; what is superposed is the clock (dynamics). Time becomes definite where it is recorded.

### Indefinite causal order

Oreshkov–Costa–Brukner (2012); the quantum switch. Worked: A = X, B = Z, control |+⟩; output ZX|ψ⟩ ⊗ |−⟩, so one use of each operation reveals anticommutation, which no definite-order circuit can do with certainty. Oreshkov (2019): relative to time-delocalized subsystems, a circuit with a causal cycle. "Indefinite Causal Order and Quantum Coordinates" (_PRL_ 135, 2025): definiteness of causal order is invariant under quantum coordinate changes; whether it comes from delocalized events or superposed metrics is a coordinate choice; the optical switch is a realization, not a simulation. 2026: non-destructive measurement inside a switch, keeping order indefinite only by erasing the ancilla's which-path information. _Positions:_ the 2025 result is the hole argument in quantum form, with the same resolution. **Causal order is definite where it is recorded.**

### The clock as observer (2025–26)

**No clock, no observables.** Without a time-shift mode to serve as a clock, boost invariance alone gives a trivial algebra in de Sitter (covariant-observer analysis, late 2025). The clock is the condition for any observables; Pauli's bound makes the entropy finite. Observer models now include observers emerging from inflationary fields, fully quantized particle clocks, and Goldstone fields; each still chooses its observer degrees of freedom in advance.

**The tracial question.** CLPW's maximum-entropy state is empty de Sitter tensored with a thermal clock energy distribution. Worked: in ρ = diag(0.8, 0.2), ⟨σ₊σ₋⟩ = 0.8 and ⟨σ₋σ₊⟩ = 0.2, ratio e^{βε}; in the maximally mixed state both 0.5. A trace, ω(ab) = ω(ba), has no ordering asymmetry: no orientation, trivial thermal time. _Position:_ in exact empty de Sitter, **no correlation can say which way the observer's clock runs**: recordable time requires a departure from maximum entropy. Recent work: the no-boundary state is the maximum-entropy state (_JHEP_ 11, 2025), and only exact de Sitter admits KMS on a worldline; with a dynamical cosmological constant (inflaton zero mode) no maximum-entropy state exists and the algebra is plausibly type II∞; a July 2026 preprint argues that beyond a scrambling time horizon shockwaves break KMS and the Hartle–Hawking state is **not a trace** on the crossed product. _Position:_ "no time without a gradient" is supported where the question is well posed (exact de Sitter, sub-scrambling times), not yet testable beyond. **Flag on _Space_ §6:** the CLPW tracial maximum-entropy state is established in its model but under active challenge as of mid-2026.

**Changing clocks changes the entropy** (De Vuyst–Eccles–Höhn–Kirklin, _JHEP_ 07, 2025): different quantum reference frames give different crossed products and different entropies; clock–field entanglement gives linear corrections to S\_gen; four cases include monotonic against periodic clocks, the global time problem reappearing as an entropy difference. _Position:_ **different clocks keep different books.** Frames are related by explicit maps (relative, not conventional), but they relate cuts; they do not select one.

**Does physics pick out where records form?** Quantum Darwinism explains objectivity given a split, and was observed directly in superconducting circuits (_Science Advances_, 2025). Quantum mereology (Carroll–Singh 2021) selects factorizations by robustness and predictability; a 2026 _PRD_ paper finds that for a fixed Hamiltonian several factorizations can qualify, not necessarily aligned with locality. Three grades: from H alone, no; from H plus a quasiclassicality criterion, narrowed to a family; with the state as well, plausibly, not shown. _Position:_ the narrowing criterion is a criterion about records, the same demand that, through a low-entropy state, gives the arrow.

### The bet, tested

_The clock–world cut cannot be derived from H and ψ alone; a clock is a subsystem whose ticks are irreversible, redundant records; so the arrow of time and the observer cut are one assumption, not two._

1. **Time as correlation with a clock: confirmed, strengthened.** In a closed universe, no clock means no observables.
2. **The cut not derivable from H and ψ: confirmed, refined.** H alone selects nothing; a quasiclassicality criterion narrows the cut to a family; quantum reference frames relate its members without choosing. **Constrained, not derived.**
3. **Clocks as irreversible records: revised and supported.** The clockwork can be nearly reversible; a shared tick is a record and costs entropy.
4. **Arrow and cut as one assumption: half-broken,** as _Space_'s bet was. Not one assumption, but **joined at one requirement, the possibility of records.** The arrow needs a low-entropy state so records can accumulate; the cut needs records to be robust and predictable; with no departure from maximum entropy, nothing recordable has an orientation. The requirement is an input to physics, not an output.

**The working claim, broken three times.**

1. _Not one:_ simultaneity relative to a reference system; one proper time per worldline. **Overturned.**
2. _Not external:_ the Hamiltonian a constraint; time a correlation relative to a division, along the state's modular flow where locally thermal. **Overturned.**
3. _The clock not free:_ bounded below and so imperfect; in a closed universe the condition for any observables; a shared tick costs entropy; different clocks assign different entropies. **Replaced by a physical subsystem with a price.**

**Endpoint.** Is time a parameter, a relation, or what a record-keeping subsystem is? _Position:_ **all three, at three levels.** Fundamentally a relation; approximately a parameter, in regimes; and the time anyone can share is what record-keeping subsystems produce, at an entropy cost, in the direction set by the gradient that makes records possible. Augustine's present of past things was close to the physical answer, with the records moved out of the mind and given a price.

## 7. The single line of thought

One question, asked with steadily less confidence that the world supplies the answer: what is it that change is counted against, and who can share the count?

Computation kept the step. Space kept the relation. Time kept the _correlation with a clock_, and lost the universal flow, the external parameter, and the free clock, everything except the records that let a tick be shared.

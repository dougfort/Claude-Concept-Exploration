# Concept Genealogies

_A research program: tracing a concept from intuition, through the fight over formalizing it, to the point where the formalization turns out to be more real than the intuition._

## Method

Each exploration follows the same arc:

1. **The intuition** — where the idea first shows up, before anyone has mathematics for it.
2. **The dispute** — the argument that breaks out when people try to make it precise, and what concepts were missing that made it unresolvable.
3. **The formalization** — who resolved it, with what machinery, and what the resolution cost.
4. **The abstraction** — what the concept looks like once the original physical picture is stripped away.
5. **The payoff** — where the abstract version turns out to describe something the intuition never could.

Working conventions:

* Genealogy and abstract shape first; derivations only when they clarify a concept.
* Take stances on disputed questions; mark them as positions, not results.
* One stage per message, with steering between stages.
* Settle the arc before stage 1 and save it as a short outline file; open the outline with a working claim for the genealogy to break. (Adopted in the measurement exploration.)
* End each exploration with a markdown summary saved to the project, in the format of the existing ones.
* Detours that outgrow a stage become side files rather than sections.
* Worked mathematical examples in plain terms, with small numbers. (Adopted in the computation exploration.)
* Interests that span several explorations become side files written at the close, not detours mid-stage. (Adopted in the space exploration.)
* Before writing stage 5, search for developments from the last two years on the stage's specific threads, so the payoff isn't built only on prior knowledge. Report what changed the argument, and flag anything recent and untested. (Adopted in the time exploration.)
* A dense stage may be split into two messages (4a/4b, 5a/5b) when its closing question needs room. (Adopted in the time exploration.)
* When a plain-terms construction is needed that earlier explorations used without building, build it on the smallest example first (one qubit, then two). (Adopted in the time exploration.)
* Name side-file candidates in the outline and settle which will be written before stage 2, so stages can defer technical lineages to them. (Adopted in the information exploration.)
* When a worked example is computational, deliver a small runnable script (pure Python, no libraries), quote its output in the stage, and reuse it across stages and side files. (Adopted in the information exploration.)
* One conversation per topic.

## Completed

### Waves — _what-is-a-wave.md_

Vibrating string (d'Alembert, Euler, Bernoulli) → Fourier and the century of rigor that built L² → the abstract wave as locality → Faraday, Maxwell, Heaviside → the wave function → quantum fields. Endpoint: a wave is a persisting pattern under local rules, with progressively less insistence that anything be underneath it.

### Symmetry — _what-is-symmetry.md_

Euclid's five solids and the crystallographers → Galois's unsolvable quintic and the group as "operation as object" → Lie's generators → the action principle and Noether's theorem → gauge theory, where demanding a local symmetry forces a field with Maxwell's dynamics → spontaneous breaking and the Higgs mechanism. Endpoint: symmetry begins as a property of things and ends as a property of descriptions, at which point it becomes geometry and tells the object what to be. Position taken: gauge symmetry is a redundancy, and the physical content is the connection.

### Entropy — _what-is-entropy.md_

Carnot's waterfall and Clausius's δQ/T → the Boltzmann–Loschmidt–Zermelo dispute and the Past Hypothesis → Gibbs, Shannon, and Jaynes's statistical mechanics as inference → Szilard, Landauer, Bennett: the demon pays when it forgets → entanglement entropy, black holes, the area law, and gravity as an equation of state. Endpoint: entropy is a property of a division between tracked and untracked variables; where we choose the division it is relative to us, where nature imposes it (a horizon) it is as objective as mass. Position taken: "system or knowledge" is a false dichotomy, like asking whether velocity belongs to the object.

### Randomness — _what-is-randomness.md_

The lot, Aristotle against Epicurus, and the aleatory contract → the three-round dispute over what a probability is a property of (Bernoulli/Bayes/Laplace; Venn and Bertrand; Fisher/Neyman against Ramsey/de Finetti/Jeffreys/Jaynes) → Kolmogorov's measure, which formalized probability by exiling randomness to a null set, with von Mises as the road not taken → algorithmic randomness: Kolmogorov complexity, Martin-Löf tests, the Schnorr–Levin equivalence, Chaitin's Ω and incompleteness as the generic case → Gleason and Kochen–Specker, Bell, certified randomness, and the measurement problem as the schism purified. Endpoint: randomness is what is absent from a description, and the genealogy strips away in turn the cause, the reason, the sequence, the sample space, and the definite outcome. Positions taken: the frequentist's chance is the Noether charge of the Bayesian's exchangeability; Kolmogorov formalized probability, not randomness; Chaitin's theorem is right and his gloss overreaches; Bell settles Epicurus against Democritus conditional on locality and free choice; the interpretations of quantum mechanics are the round-3 positions with the Laplacian middle removed.

### Measurement — _what-is-measurement.md_

The cubit and the metre bar, Eudoxus to Helmholtz and Hölder, Leibniz's compresence and Kant's hands, Weyl's rod and Einstein's atoms, the 2019 SI → von Neumann's chain and the movable cut, proper vs. improper mixtures, Schrödinger's cat, Wigner's friend, Deutsch's reversible friend, DLP irreversibility and Bell's FAPP → POVMs and Naimark dilation; decoherence (Zeh, Joos–Zeh), einselection, and quantum Darwinism, with redundancy as the definition of a record → Everett (measurement as correlation, outcomes as indexical) against QBism (measurement as an agent's act, the urgleichung) → Local Friendliness, black-hole complementarity, AMPS, and Harlow–Hayden. Working claim broken: measurement is comparison under a transported convention — transport repaired (atoms, the SI), non-disturbing comparison reconstructed as a regime (redundant records), universal comparability not repaired (friends, horizons). Endpoint: a measurement result is a record, and physics is increasingly a theory of which records can be brought together, by whom, and at what cost. Positions taken: the 2019 SI vindicates Duff — fixing dimensionful constants is a gauge choice; Bohr identified the missing concept (the record) and wrongly made it a primitive; von Neumann's consistency theorem did more harm than good; Boolean event structure is what survives copying; quantum Darwinism solves objectivity, not outcomes, and the cut has become a factorization; Everett is the stronger abstraction of measurement, QBism of probability, and QBism repeats Bohr's move; observed events are absolute to the degree their records are redundant; what can be compared is bounded by what can be computed. Tentative: Everett and QBism are complementary views of one structure.

### Computation — _what-is-computation.md_

Llull and Leibniz's calculemus, Boole's x² = x, Frege's gapless proof, Prony's divided labour, Babbage and Lovelace → Hilbert's _Entscheidungsproblem_, the diagonal escape from every class of total functions, and the 1936 contest of definitions (Church, Gödel–Herbrand, Post, Turing), won by Turing's analysis of the clerk → the stored-program computer: analog, relay, and tube lineages, ENIAC's setup problem, the EDVAC report and the priority dispute, Turing's ACE, memory technology, the first runs, and code-as-data from self-modification to W^X → complexity: Gödel's 1956 letter, Hartmanis–Stearns, Cobham–Edmonds, the extended Church–Turing thesis, Cook–Levin–Karp, the relativization and natural-proofs barriers, pseudorandomness and cryptography, Landauer–Bennett and the Toffoli gate → Feynman and Deutsch, interference as the source of quantum speedup, Shor worked for N = 15, the state of hardware in 2026, and the physical Church–Turing thesis. Working claim broken: computation is what a clerk does by finite rules, a fact of logic independent of physics — relocated (the thesis is a theorem with physical premises), overturned (feasibility depends on physics), extended (hardness as a physical boundary). Endpoint: a computation is a physical process viewed through a description that says which of its features count as symbols. Positions taken: undecidability is the price of a closed definition; the Church–Turing thesis is a theorem whose premises describe a physical agent; "von Neumann architecture" names the least inventive and most consequential contribution, the abstraction layer; code-as-data grew in software and was fenced off in hardware; polynomial time is the class fixed by closure under composition and change of machine, and "feasible" is a loose label; the hardness of proving hardness is a consequence of hardness; SAT solvers show what worst-case theory is about, not its failure; quantum speedup is hidden-symmetry detection by interference; the extended thesis is false (conditionally); physics is not computation, computation is a physical category.

### Space — _what-is-space.md_

Parmenides' void and the atomists, Aristotle's place, Euclid without space, Descartes's coordinates, Newton's bucket, Leibniz, Euler, Kant's Amphiboly → the parallel postulate from Proclus through Khayyam, Saccheri, Lambert, and Legendre to Gauss, Bolyai, Lobachevsky, and Beltrami's models; Helmholtz and Poincaré → Gauss's _Theorema egregium_, Riemann's 1854 lecture, Levi-Civita's transport, Minkowski, general relativity, the hole argument → topology, Gelfand–Naimark and Connes; tensor factors vs. subalgebras, type III local algebras, Reeh–Schlieder, the split property, centres at gauge cuts, and the factorization problem (Zanardi, Cotler–Penington–Ranard, Carroll–Singh) → Ryu–Takayanagi, error correction (three-qutrit code), Harlow's area-as-centre, islands, complexity = volume, Donnelly–Giddings, the crossed product, and CLPW's observer in de Sitter. Working claim broken: space is the given arena that divides the world into parts — the arena stops being given, places lose identity, parts stop coming from places. Endpoint: space is the pattern of correlation among observables available relative to a reference system; connectivity is entanglement, depth is redundancy, area is a shared record. Positions taken: Aristotle's anti-void argument is valid and his dynamics fails; Newton wins the bucket on his own terms; Riemann replaced "which geometry is true" with "what field is the geometry"; gravity is geometry because it is universal (against Poincaré, via the identity of indiscernibles); points of spacetime are gauge — Newton right that space acts, Leibniz right that points have no identity; a space is its algebra plus an operator; a factorization is a property of an algebra of observables, not of Hilbert space; the centre at a cut is the record (tentative); holography moves the factorization problem to the boundary rather than solving it; a gravitating region's algebra is defined relative to a clock outside it, boundary or observer; the factorization problem reduces to a single cut, observer versus world, which is von Neumann's. _Flag added during the time exploration:_ CLPW's tracial maximum-entropy state is established in its model but under active challenge as of mid-2026 (dynamical cosmological constant; a scrambling-time preprint arguing the Hartle–Hawking state is not a trace). See _what-is-time.md_ §6.

### Time — _what-is-time.md_

Temporal hours, Parmenides, Zeno's arrow, the _Timaeus_, Aristotle's number of motion, Augustine's three presents, eternity and the beginning, the escapement, Oresme and Galileo, Huygens and the equation of time, Harrison, Newton's absolute time, Leibniz, Kant → railway and telegraph synchronization, Mach, Lorentz's local time, Poincaré's 1898 essay, Einstein's 1905 definition, Galison (disputed), Reichenbach against Malament, McTaggart and the block in one paragraph → Minkowski's proper time and the twin paradox, the clock hypothesis and the muon ring, gravitational time dilation and GPS, cosmic time, Gödel's universe, ADM and the vanishing Hamiltonian → Wheeler–DeWitt and the frozen formalism, Kuchař and Isham, relational observables; Pauli's theorem, Page–Wootters, Giovannetti–Lloyd–Maccone, the trinity; modular theory, Bisognano–Wichmann, Connes–Rovelli thermal time → the thermodynamic cost of timekeeping (linear bound, the 2025 ring clock, the 2025 cost of reading a clock), clocks in superposition, indefinite causal order and quantum coordinates, no clock no observables in de Sitter, the tracial-state question, observer-dependent gravitational entropy, quantum mereology. Working claim broken: time is the parameter of change, one external order the same for everything — not one (relative simultaneity, proper time per worldline), not external (time evolution as gauge; time as correlation with a clock), the clock not free (bounded below, the condition for observables, a shared tick costs entropy). Endpoint: time is fundamentally a relation to a clock, approximately a parameter in regimes, and the time anyone can share is what record-keeping subsystems produce at an entropy cost. Positions taken: Russell answers Zeno's arrow for kinematics, phase space for dynamics; Aristotle's single time is valid and rests on simultaneity; Augustine had the right structure (the past as present records) in the wrong place; the escapement mattered more than Newton; Newton's absolute time is the equation of time made a principle, and the law defines the time; Einstein's contribution was subtraction; simultaneity is relative, not conventional (Malament); the twin paradox is path length, not acceleration; proper time is geometry, being a clock is dynamics; cosmic time is absolute time returned as a property of the state; Gödel's modal step fails; the vanishing Hamiltonian, the hole argument, and no local energy are one fact; time is fundamentally a relation and a parameter in regimes; Pauli's theorem says no clock is ideal; the problem of time is the factorization problem with one factor named "clock"; two-time questions need records in the state (tentative); thermal time is correct where the state is locally thermal; canonical gravity, quantum clocks, and thermal time are one answer with three components; ticking need not be irreversible, a shared tick must be; causal order is definite where recorded; in a closed universe no clock means no observables; different clocks keep different books; the cut is constrained by the demand for records, not derived. Bet half-broken: the arrow and the cut are not one assumption but are joined at one requirement, the possibility of records.

### Information — _what-is-information.md_

_In-formare_ and the signet ring, Augustine's natural sign, the beacon and Polybius's torch alphabet, al-Kindi's frequency analysis, Bacon's biliteral cipher, Chappe and Morse → meaning against measure: Fisher information, Shannon and Weaver's three levels, Wiener's sign as stock against flow, MacKay, Bar-Hillel–Carnap and the contradiction paradox, Dretske and Floridi capped → Hamming's syndrome, the data processing inequality and regeneration, relative entropy as the primitive, rate–distortion, Kolmogorov's bridge → aboutness as correlation (mutual information), Bennett's copy against the overwrite, the repetition code, the direction of aboutness from preparation history; no-cloning, Holevo, superdense coding and teleportation, negative conditional entropy, the bit-flip code → reconstructions of quantum theory (CBH, GPT shapes, Hardy, Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti, Masanes–Müller, PR boxes, information causality, 2025–26 preprints on its reach), information conservation, constructor theory and the gravity test, records and holographic codes. Runnable companions: _hamming.py_, _qsim.py_, _gpt.py_, _shor.py_, _surface.py_ (kept outside the project). Working claim broken: information is the content of a message, about something, independent of its carrier — not content (relative to a supplied ensemble), not intrinsically about (correlation plus preparation history), not carrier-independent (true only in the copyable sector, where reversible copying exists). Endpoint: classical information is the record, the copyable sector of correlation; information in general is more; the possibility of records remains an input, resolved into three: symmetry (correlating dynamics), composition (well-defined parts), and a low-entropy gradient (blanks). Positions taken: carrier-independence always meant "given a carrier capable of the distinctions"; Polybius's move from messages to symbols was decisive; Fisher and Shannon measure one distinguishability at two scales; Shannon measures a flow, Wiener a stock; meaning lives in what selects the ensemble; Hamming's contribution is the syndrome; a record degrades unless regenerated, and regeneration is an irreversible decision; relative entropy is the primitive and entropy a special case; rate–distortion readmits meaning as a parameter; aboutness is mutual information plus a preparation history, and its direction is the thermodynamic arrow; classical information is the sector an interaction can copy; a quantum state is a description, not a stock of retrievable bits; entanglement is correlation with nothing to be about; quantum error correction is the copyable sector protecting the uncopyable; no-cloning is the definition of non-classical, not a quantum principle; one communication principle can't single out quantum correlations in general; quantum theory is information plus symmetry plus composition, and information is its language, not its source; conservation makes records costly, not possible; reversible copying is a substantive physical property; interoperability is the copyable sector stated as a law. Bet confirmed, more strongly than _Space_'s or _Time_'s.

### Proof

Euclid → the 19th-century rigor crisis → Frege and Russell → Hilbert's formalism → Gödel and Gentzen → Curry–Howard and proof assistants → computer-checked mathematics and the natural-proofs barrier. Space handed off Hilbert's _Grundlagen_ and the Frege–Hilbert dispute over consistency and existence; _mathematical-platonism.md_ is a second entry point. Computation supplied Gödel's letter (checking vs. finding), the "unprovable of the individual" thread, and the Curry–Howard side file. Information adds MIP\* = RE (interactive proofs with entangled provers verify any recursively enumerable statement) and Chaitin's information-theoretic incompleteness.

### The continuum

Zeno → Newton's fluxions and Berkeley's "ghosts of departed quantities" → Cauchy, Weierstrass, Dedekind → Cantor → Brouwer's intuitionist revolt → Robinson's nonstandard analysis. Live question: is the real line a discovery or a construction? Touched from randomness (countable vs. finite additivity, Borel normality), measurement (Eudoxus, Hölder), computation (analog computing and infinite precision as the excluded "unreasonable" model; Brouwer again via BHK), space (whether space is a manifold at all; discreteness proposals), time (Zeno's arrow; the actual-infinity dispute over the world's beginning; whether time is continuous), and information (Fisher information on a continuous parameter; infinite precision as infinite information; the continuity axiom in reconstructions).

### The function
